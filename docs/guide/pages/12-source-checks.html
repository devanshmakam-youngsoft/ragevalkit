How it works

  1. The pipeline reports its sources

    In the retrieval step, s.sources = [...] lists the documents it used.

  2. The test case lists the expected ones

    In the expected_sources column, with two small rules below.

  3. ragevalkit compares them

    Each question shows what was found and what is missing, and the version page counts them.

The two rules

You writeIt means
refunds.pdfthat document must be retrieved
shipping.pdf | tracking.pdfboth are needed (| means AND)
tracking.pdf , tracking_v2.pdfeither is enough (, means OR)
a.pdf , a_v2.pdf | policy.pdf(a or a_v2) and policy

Two ways to compare names

source_check_modeBehaviour
"keyword" (default)Fast and predictable. Ignores case, folders and file extensions, so docs/Refunds.PDF matches refunds.
"llm"Asks the judge model, which also copes with different spellings of a name. Adds a score and a short comment.
"off"No source checking.
If sources say “not checked”

Either the test case has no expected_sources, the mode is "off", or no step reported sources. Remember that decorators cannot report sources. Troubleshooting has the full list.
